OpenCamBridge privacy policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026. Publisher: xHydra / Taha Laachari. Contact: opencambridge@xhydra.fr.

Local camera, local controls

OpenCamBridge sends camera video directly from your Android phone to your Windows PC over your local network or a USB/ADB connection. The application has no cloud video relay, cloud recording service, developer account, advertising SDK or analytics/telemetry upload. The developer does not receive your camera video, pairing credentials or diagnostic logs automatically.

Video is processed in memory and the Windows local frame ring. The app does not offer persistent video recording; an application you select as a camera consumer may record or transmit it under that application's policies. Local mapped-frame storage may leave operating-system-managed disk/cache remnants. Do not interpret "no recording feature" as forensic secure deletion.

What stays on your devices

The app stores preferences, saved-device names/network endpoints and credentials needed to reconnect. Android protects pairing keys with its Keystore; Windows protects saved pairing credentials with DPAPI. Camera permission is used to capture video and scan pairing QR codes. Network permissions enable local discovery/control/video. A foreground camera-service notification shows active capture. The app does not capture microphone audio or read contacts or location for this webcam workflow.

Diagnostic logs may contain device/camera names, IP addresses, settings, timing and errors. Windows keeps local logs under C:\ProgramData\OpenCamBridge; app preferences/pairings are stored locally. Logs are not uploaded automatically. If you email logs or contact support, the information you choose to send is received through the email provider and used to respond and troubleshoot.

Network security boundaries

Pairing protects its credential exchange. The current video/control transport uses HTTP on your trusted LAN, not HTTPS; it must not be described as encrypted video or safe for hostile/public Wi-Fi. LAN control requires a token. USB-only mode binds the phone server to loopback. The installer restricts its Windows pairing firewall exception to private networks and the local subnet. Do not expose listening ports on your router.

External services you choose to open

Windows can check public GitHub release metadata on request or once at startup if you enable that option (off by default). These HTTPS requests expose your ordinary network IP address to GitHub; they contain no camera video, phone details, pairing token, diagnostic log or unique app identifier. Updates are never installed silently. This optional request is not a cloud camera service.

Website/documentation, Google Play reviews, GitHub, support email and optional Ko-fi links open external apps/sites when selected. Those providers can receive normal connection/account information under their own privacy policies. They are not camera relays. Windows installation may download WebView2 from Microsoft if needed. Platform stores and the operating system may independently process installation, crash, security and account information.

Review dismissal is a local preference. Opening a review link is not evidence that you submitted a rating; OpenCamBridge does not receive your rating history. Optional donations provide no app feature or digital entitlement.

Control, retention and deletion

Stop capture to stop sending new camera frames. Forget a phone on Windows or revoke a PC on Android to remove/revoke the pairing. Clear app storage or remove the Android app to delete app-private data. The Windows uninstaller preserves user preferences, pairings and logs; delete those local files if you want to remove them, taking care not to delete another user's files. Data you send to another camera consumer or external provider is governed by that provider.

Contact opencambridge@xhydra.fr for privacy questions or deletion of information you sent directly to support. Policy changes will be published with an updated effective date. This product is a general-purpose webcam tool, not an app aimed at children.
