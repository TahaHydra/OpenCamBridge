## English (en-US)

Short description (under 80 characters):

Turn your Android phone into a Windows webcam over Wi-Fi or USB

Full description:

Use your Android camera in your Windows camera applications with OpenCamBridge.
Install the Windows companion, connect both devices on your trusted local
network, pair them, and choose OpenCamBridge Camera in your video application.

• Local video: camera frames travel directly between your phone and PC.
• QR-code or short-code pairing, with saved devices and revoke controls.
• Desktop preview and camera controls, including supported lenses, zoom and torch.
• Independent output framing: select the crop your Windows camera apps receive.
• H.264 streaming with an MJPEG compatibility option.
• Optional USB connection through Android platform-tools and USB debugging.
• No developer account, ads, watermark or paid feature unlocks.

Requires Android 10 or newer and the OpenCamBridge companion for Windows 11 x64.
Available resolutions and frame rates depend on your device, lens and Windows
camera application. Not every phone supports 60 FPS. This release sends video,
not microphone audio. The Windows companion is downloaded separately.

Wi-Fi mode is intended for trusted private networks. Pairing protects credential
exchange; current LAN video/control uses HTTP rather than encrypted video.
No cloud camera relay or automatic video/log upload is provided.

Get the companion and instructions: https://xhydra.fr/products/opencambridge
Questions or problems: opencambridge@xhydra.fr
